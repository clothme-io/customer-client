## About ClothME

ClothME is building the future of fashion commerce.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform combines personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For merchants, ClothME offers a performance-based model designed to help businesses acquire customers, increase sales, and reduce fit-related returns—without upfront setup, subscription, or listing fees.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company building for a global market, and we're looking for exceptional engineers who want to help shape that future.

## About the Role

We're looking for a **Founding Senior Mobile Engineer** to join our early engineering team and help build the next generation of ClothME's mobile applications.

You'll work directly with our Founder and CEO/CTO to develop, improve, and scale our mobile experiences across iOS and Android.

ClothME already has a working mobile application built with React Native and Expo. Your responsibility will be to understand the existing codebase, improve its architecture and performance, and help evolve it into a reliable, highly engaging shopping experience capable of supporting millions of users.

This is a hands-on engineering position with significant technical ownership and influence over the mobile product.

You'll collaborate closely with backend, AI, product design, and growth teams to deliver features that make fashion discovery more personal, intuitive, and enjoyable.

We're looking for someone who cares deeply about user experience, enjoys solving challenging mobile engineering problems, and takes pride in shipping high-quality applications.

## Interview Process

Our interview process is designed to understand your mobile engineering experience, technical judgment, collaboration, and approach to building exceptional user experiences.

**Stage 1 — Initial Conversation**

Discuss your background, mobile development experience, and interest in ClothME.

**Stage 2 — Founder & CTO Interview**

Meet directly with the Founder to discuss ClothME's product vision, mobile architecture, and engineering challenges.

**Stage 3 — Technical Architecture Discussion**

Explore React Native architecture, Expo, state management, navigation, performance optimization, native integrations, and mobile reliability.

**Stage 4 — Practical Engineering Exercise**

Complete a focused, job-relevant exercise or review an existing project that demonstrates your mobile engineering skills. We aim to respect candidates' time and avoid unnecessarily lengthy assignments.

**Stage 5 — Collaboration & Product Thinking**

Discuss how you approach user experience, collaborate with designers, handle ambiguity, and make engineering trade-offs.

**Stage 6 — References & Offer**

For shortlisted candidates, complete reference checks and discuss compensation, equity, and next steps.
