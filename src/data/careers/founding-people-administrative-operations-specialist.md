## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding People & Administrative Operations Specialist** who wants to help build a supportive, organized, and efficient workplace as ClothME grows.

## About the Role

As a Founding People & Administrative Operations Specialist, you'll help establish and maintain the people and administrative processes that support ClothME's employees and day-to-day business operations.

You'll work directly with our Founder and CEO and collaborate with employees, hiring teams, external service providers, and professional advisers.

This is a hands-on position combining recruitment coordination, employee onboarding, people administration, payroll and benefits coordination, office operations, vendor administration, and employee experience.

You'll help ensure that every employee has a positive experience from their first interaction with ClothME through onboarding, professional development, and day-to-day employment.

You'll also help build the internal administrative systems that allow our teams to focus on developing products, serving customers, and growing the company.

We're not looking for someone who simply processes paperwork.

We want someone who enjoys helping people, creating organized systems, solving problems, and building an excellent workplace.

This role does not require you to act as an accountant, attorney, or senior HR executive. You'll coordinate with qualified professionals whenever specialized legal, accounting, employment, or compliance expertise is needed.

As ClothME grows, you'll have opportunities to develop professionally, take on broader responsibilities, and contribute to building our future people and administrative operations.

## Your Mission

Help create a workplace where talented people feel supported, administrative processes work efficiently, and ClothME can recruit, onboard, and retain exceptional employees as the company grows.

## Interview Process

Our interview process focuses on practical experience, organizational ability, communication, judgment, and the ability to support employees effectively.

**Stage 1 — Initial Conversation**

Discuss your background, people operations experience, administrative skills, and interest in ClothME.

**Stage 2 — Founder Interview**

Meet with the Founder to learn about ClothME's mission, team culture, hiring priorities, and administrative needs.

**Stage 3 — People & Administrative Operations Discussion**

Walk us through recruitment processes, employee onboarding, administrative systems, or workplace improvements you've personally helped coordinate.

**Stage 4 — Practical Administrative Scenario**

Complete a focused, job-relevant exercise involving a realistic recruitment, employee onboarding, payroll coordination, or administrative challenge.

We aim to respect candidates' time and avoid unnecessarily lengthy assignments.

**Stage 5 — Collaboration & Values Interview**

Discuss your approach to confidentiality, communication, employee support, teamwork, prioritization, and problem-solving.

**Stage 6 — References & Offer**

For shortlisted candidates, complete reference checks and discuss compensation, responsibilities, and next steps.
