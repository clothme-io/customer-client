## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding AI Engineer** who wants to help build the intelligence behind that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding AI Engineer, you'll play a central role in developing and improving the artificial intelligence systems powering ClothME.

You'll work directly with our Founder and CEO/CTO and collaborate closely with Backend Engineering, Mobile Engineering, Product, and Design to build intelligent systems that make fashion discovery more personal, accurate, and useful.

This is a hands-on engineering position combining applied machine learning, software engineering, experimentation, and production AI development.

You'll help improve our fit intelligence capabilities, build recommendation and ranking systems, develop personalized shopping experiences, and explore new applications of AI across our marketplace.

You'll also help establish the technical foundation for future AI-powered products and services as ClothME grows.

We're not looking for someone who only wants to experiment with models.

We want someone who enjoys turning research and ideas into reliable products that solve real problems.

As ClothME expands, there will be opportunities to specialize, take ownership of larger technical initiatives, mentor other engineers, and grow into senior technical or leadership positions.

## Your Mission

Build and improve the intelligence that helps people discover fashion likely to fit them, supports better shopping decisions, and enables merchants to reach relevant customers.
