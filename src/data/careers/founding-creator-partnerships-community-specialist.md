## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Alongside ClothME, we're developing **Thesi**, our creator platform designed to connect creators with opportunities to promote fashion products, participate in campaigns, produce authentic content, and earn through their work.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding Creator Partnerships & Community Specialist** who wants to help build that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Creator Partnerships & Community Specialist, you'll play an important role in building and supporting ClothME's creator ecosystem.

You'll work directly with our Founder and collaborate with Growth & Marketing, Merchant Partnerships, Product, and Engineering to recruit creators, help them onboard to Thesi, coordinate campaigns, and develop an engaged creator community.

This is a hands-on role combining creator recruitment, community building, partnerships, campaign coordination, content strategy, and creator success.

You'll help creators understand how to participate in campaigns, create engaging content, promote products, and build opportunities to earn through the platform.

You'll also help us understand what creators need, identify ways to improve their experience, and develop programs that encourage long-term participation.

We're not simply looking to build a large list of influencers.

We want to build a community of creators who are active, supported, and genuinely excited about the opportunities available through ClothME and Thesi.

As ClothME grows, there will be opportunities to develop professionally, specialize in different areas, and take on broader responsibilities.

## Your Mission

Help build a thriving creator community where creators can discover opportunities, produce authentic fashion content, grow their influence, and generate meaningful value for themselves, merchants, and shoppers.
