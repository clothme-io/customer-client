## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model. There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand into international markets.

We're looking for a **Founding Growth & Marketing Specialist** who wants to help build that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Growth & Marketing Specialist, you'll play a central role in introducing ClothME to consumers, fashion brands, boutiques, and creators.

You'll work directly with our Founder and collaborate closely with product, engineering, merchant partnerships, and creator community teams to develop and execute strategies that help ClothME grow.

This is a hands-on position.

You'll be involved in planning campaigns, creating content, experimenting with acquisition channels, building relationships, analyzing results, and continuously improving our growth strategies.

You'll have the opportunity to contribute ideas, test new approaches, and take ownership of meaningful projects.

As ClothME grows, there will be opportunities to develop professionally, specialize in areas of interest, and take on broader responsibilities.

We're looking for someone who enjoys building, learning, experimenting, and making a measurable impact.
