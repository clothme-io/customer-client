## About ClothME

ClothME is building the future of fashion commerce.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform combines personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For merchants, ClothME offers a performance-based model designed to help businesses acquire customers, increase sales, and reduce fit-related returns—without upfront setup, subscription, or listing fees.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company building for a global market, and we're looking for exceptional engineers who want to help shape that future.

## About the Role

We're looking for a **Founding Senior Backend Engineer** to join our early engineering team and help build the technology infrastructure behind ClothME.

You'll work directly with our Founder and CEO/CTO to develop, improve, and scale the backend systems supporting our consumer marketplace, merchant platform, creator ecosystem, and AI-powered shopping experiences.

ClothME already has a working platform. Your responsibility will be to understand the existing architecture, improve its reliability and performance, and help evolve it into a secure, scalable global commerce platform.

This is a hands-on engineering position with significant technical ownership and influence over architectural decisions.

We're looking for someone who enjoys solving difficult problems, takes pride in writing excellent software, and wants to help build a technology company from its earliest stages.

## What You'll Do

### Backend Engineering & Architecture

- Design, develop, maintain, and optimize backend services and APIs.
- Improve the architecture, reliability, and scalability of our existing systems.
- Build maintainable, well-tested, production-ready code.
- Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks.
- Design efficient database schemas, queries, and data-access patterns.
- Develop reusable services and components.
- Participate in technical planning and architecture decisions.
- Help establish engineering standards, documentation, and development practices.

### Marketplace & Commerce Infrastructure

Build and improve the services powering:

- Consumer accounts and family Fit Profiles.
- Merchant onboarding and brand verification.
- Product catalogs, variants, sizes, and inventory.
- Product discovery and fit-based matching.
- Shopping carts, checkout, orders, and payments.
- Merchant commissions and transaction records.
- Order status, cancellations, refunds, and returns.
- Notifications and customer engagement.
- Merchant dashboards and operational tools.

### Integrations & External Platforms

- Build and maintain integrations with Shopify and other commerce platforms.
- Develop secure payment workflows using Stripe and supported payment providers.
- Implement reliable product and inventory synchronization.
- Design webhook processing and background jobs.
- Build integrations with print-on-demand, fulfillment, and shipping providers.
- Develop APIs supporting future third-party integrations.

### Creator Commerce

Support the integration between ClothME and its creator ecosystem, Thesi.

Responsibilities may include:

- Creator attribution and referral tracking.
- Product campaigns and creator storefronts.
- Affiliate links and deep-link attribution.
- Commission calculations and transaction tracking.
- Creator campaign reporting.
- Reliable data exchange between services.

### Performance, Reliability & Security

- Optimize API response times and database performance.
- Implement caching and efficient background processing.
- Improve monitoring, logging, and observability.
- Design systems that handle increasing traffic and transaction volumes.
- Implement appropriate authentication, authorization, and data protection.
- Protect sensitive customer, merchant, and transaction information.
- Improve fault tolerance, recovery procedures, and operational reliability.
- Identify and address security vulnerabilities.

### Engineering Collaboration

- Work closely with mobile, frontend, AI, and product engineering.
- Collaborate with the Founder on technical architecture and priorities.
- Review code and provide constructive technical feedback.
- Participate in debugging, testing, and production incident resolution.
- Help future engineers onboard successfully.
- Contribute to a culture of technical excellence, ownership, and continuous improvement.

## Interview Process

Our interview process is designed to understand your engineering experience, problem-solving ability, communication, and approach to building reliable systems.

**Stage 1 — Initial Conversation**

Discuss your background, interests, and experience working on production systems.

**Stage 2 — Founder & CTO Interview**

Meet directly with the Founder to discuss ClothME, the engineering challenges we're solving, and how you approach technical decisions.

**Stage 3 — Technical Architecture Discussion**

Explore practical topics such as API design, database performance, scalable marketplace systems, concurrency, reliability, and security.

**Stage 4 — Practical Engineering Exercise**

Complete a focused, job-relevant exercise or review an existing project that demonstrates your engineering skills. We aim to respect candidates' time and avoid unnecessarily lengthy assignments.

**Stage 5 — Collaboration & Values Interview**

Discuss how you communicate, collaborate, handle ambiguity, and take ownership.

**Stage 6 — References & Offer**

For shortlisted candidates, complete reference checks and discuss compensation, equity, and next steps.
