## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding Product Designer** who wants to help shape that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Product Designer, you'll play a central role in designing how people experience ClothME.

You'll work directly with our Founder and CEO/CTO and collaborate closely with Engineering, AI, Growth, Merchant Partnerships, and Creator Partnerships to create thoughtful, intuitive, and engaging digital experiences.

This is a hands-on product design position combining user experience design, visual design, interaction design, prototyping, research, and product thinking.

You'll help improve our existing applications while designing new features and experiences across our consumer marketplace, merchant platform, and creator ecosystem.

You'll be involved throughout the product development process—from understanding user problems and exploring ideas to creating designs, testing solutions, and working with engineers to bring experiences into production.

We're not looking for someone who only creates beautiful screens.

We want someone who understands how great design helps people accomplish meaningful goals, reduces friction, builds trust, and makes complex technology feel simple.

As ClothME grows, there will be opportunities to specialize, take ownership of larger product areas, mentor other designers, and grow into senior design or leadership positions.

## Your Mission

Design simple, engaging, and accessible experiences that help people discover fashion likely to fit them, help merchants grow their businesses, and help creators participate successfully in the ClothME ecosystem.

## Interview Process

Our interview process focuses on design quality, product thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, and the ability to create useful experiences.

**Stage 1 — Initial Conversation**

Discuss your background, product design experience, and interest in ClothME.

**Stage 2 — Founder Interview**

Meet with the Founder to discuss ClothME's product vision, design priorities, and the experiences we're building.

**Stage 3 — Portfolio & Product Thinking Discussion**

Walk us through selected projects, including the problems you solved, your design process, trade-offs, and outcomes.

**Stage 4 — Practical Design Exercise**

Complete a focused, job-relevant design exercise or review an existing project demonstrating your skills. We aim to respect candidates' time and avoid unnecessarily lengthy assignments.

**Stage 5 — Collaboration & Design Delivery**

Discuss how you work with engineers, respond to feedback, handle constraints, and ensure design quality through implementation.

**Stage 6 — References & Offer**

For shortlisted candidates, complete reference checks and discuss compensation, responsibilities, and next steps.
