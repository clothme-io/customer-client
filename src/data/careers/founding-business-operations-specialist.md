## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform combines personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding Business Operations Specialist** who wants to help build the operational foundation behind that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Business Operations Specialist, you'll help ensure ClothME operates efficiently, executes its priorities, and maintains strong coordination across teams.

You'll work directly with the Founder and collaborate with Engineering, Product, Growth, Merchant Partnerships, Creator Partnerships, and Customer Experience.

This is a hands-on position focused on business execution, operational planning, cross-functional coordination, process improvement, and performance reporting.

You'll help teams stay aligned, identify operational bottlenecks, build repeatable processes, and ensure important initiatives move forward.

You won't be expected to run every department or perform the work of other specialists. Instead, you'll help the organization work effectively as a whole.

As ClothME grows, there will be opportunities to take on broader responsibilities, develop leadership skills, and contribute to increasingly important business initiatives.

## Your Mission

Build efficient operating systems and help teams execute company priorities consistently, enabling ClothME to grow without unnecessary complexity.
