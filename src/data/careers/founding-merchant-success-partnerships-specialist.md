## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand into international markets.

We're looking for a **Founding Merchant Success & Partnerships Specialist** who wants to help build that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Merchant Success & Partnerships Specialist, you'll play an important role in building and supporting the network of fashion brands, boutiques, retailers, and independent merchants that power the ClothME marketplace.

You'll work directly with our Founder and collaborate with Growth, Product, Engineering, and Customer Experience to identify prospective merchants, introduce them to ClothME, guide them through onboarding, and help them succeed on the platform.

This is a hands-on position combining business development, merchant acquisition, relationship building, onboarding, and customer success.

You'll be involved in everything from researching potential brand partners and conducting outreach to helping merchants create their storefronts, publish products, and improve their marketplace performance.

We want someone who understands that acquiring a merchant is only the beginning. The real goal is to build long-term relationships with businesses that see genuine value from ClothME.

As the company grows, there will be opportunities to develop professionally, specialize in different areas, and take on broader responsibilities.

## Your Mission

Help fashion businesses discover ClothME, join the marketplace, successfully publish their products, and build long-term relationships that create value for merchants and shoppers.

## What You'll Do

### Merchant Acquisition & Business Development

Help grow ClothME's merchant network across our launch markets.

Responsibilities include:

- Research and identify prospective fashion brands, boutiques, retailers, and independent designers.
- Develop and execute merchant outreach strategies.
- Introduce ClothME to potential merchant partners.
- Communicate the benefits of fit-first shopping and performance-based customer acquisition.
- Conduct introductory calls, product demonstrations, and partnership discussions.
- Build and maintain a qualified pipeline of prospective merchants.
- Follow up consistently with interested businesses.
- Work with Growth & Marketing on merchant acquisition campaigns.
- Identify promising fashion categories, geographic markets, and merchant segments.
- Support merchant acquisition initiatives as ClothME expands into additional cities.

### Merchant Onboarding & Activation

Help merchants successfully establish their presence on ClothME.

Responsibilities include:

- Guide merchants through account registration and initial setup.
- Help merchants create and complete their brand profiles.
- Explain brand verification requirements and submission processes.
- Support merchants as they add and manage their product catalogs.
- Help merchants understand product information, sizing, images, variants, and inventory requirements.
- Assist merchants with available store integrations.
- Troubleshoot onboarding difficulties and coordinate technical support when needed.
- Develop onboarding checklists and educational resources.
- Monitor onboarding progress and follow up with merchants who become inactive.
- Help merchants move from registration to having approved brands and published products.

### Merchant Success & Retention

Develop long-term relationships with merchants and help them receive meaningful value from the platform.

Responsibilities include:

- Maintain regular communication with active merchants.
- Understand merchant goals, challenges, and business needs.
- Help merchants improve catalog completeness and product presentation.
- Encourage merchants to maintain accurate product and inventory information.
- Educate merchants about available platform features.
- Identify opportunities to improve merchant engagement.
- Support merchants in understanding their marketplace performance.
- Coordinate with internal teams to resolve merchant concerns.
- Identify merchants at risk of becoming inactive.
- Help improve merchant satisfaction and long-term retention.

### Merchant Education & Enablement

Help make ClothME easy for fashion businesses to understand and use.

Responsibilities include:

- Create merchant onboarding guides and checklists.
- Develop educational emails and tutorials.
- Support webinars, product demonstrations, and onboarding sessions.
- Explain platform features in clear, practical language.
- Help merchants understand how fit-based product matching works.
- Communicate new features, product updates, and improvements.
- Gather common merchant questions and contribute to help resources.
- Identify opportunities to simplify the merchant experience.

### Merchant Partnerships & Industry Relationships

Help establish ClothME as a trusted partner within the fashion industry.

Responsibilities include:

**Merchant Acquisition**

**Merchant Activation**
- Percentage of merchants completing onboarding.

**Merchant Success**

- Merchant satisfaction.
- Merchant engagement.

**Marketplace Quality**
- Product catalog completeness.
- Accuracy of merchant information.
- Merchant responsiveness.
- Resolution of onboarding problems.

Specific targets will be established based on ClothME's growth stage, available resources, and business priorities.

**Stage 3 — Merchant Acquisition & Success Discussion**

Discuss your approach to teamwork, communication, customer focus, and problem-solving.
