## About ClothME

ClothME is building a better way for people to discover and shop for fashion.

We're creating a fit-first fashion marketplace that helps people discover clothing likely to fit them and their families, while helping fashion brands, boutiques, and retailers reach relevant shoppers.

Our platform brings together personalized fit intelligence, product discovery, merchant technology, and creator-driven commerce to make fashion shopping more convenient, personalized, and accessible.

For fashion businesses, ClothME provides an opportunity to acquire new customers, increase online and in-store sales, and reduce fit-related returns through a performance-based model.

There are no setup, subscription, or listing fees. ClothME earns a commission when it generates a sale.

Our long-term vision is to become the operating system for fashion retail.

We're an early-stage technology company with ambitious plans to grow across North America and eventually expand internationally.

We're looking for a **Founding Customer Experience Specialist** who wants to help build that future.

## About the Role

As a Founding Customer Experience Specialist, you'll play an important role in creating an exceptional experience for everyone who interacts with ClothME.

You'll work directly with our Founder and collaborate with Product, Engineering, Growth, Merchant Partnerships, and Creator Partnerships to help shoppers, merchants, and creators succeed on the platform.

This is a hands-on position combining customer support, problem-solving, customer education, feedback collection, and customer experience improvement.

You'll help people navigate the platform, resolve issues, understand available features, and have a positive experience with ClothME.

You'll also help us identify recurring problems, improve support processes, and turn customer feedback into meaningful product improvements.

We're not looking for someone who simply responds to tickets.

We want someone who genuinely cares about people, enjoys solving problems, and wants to help create a customer experience that people remember for the right reasons.

As ClothME grows, there will be opportunities to develop professionally, specialize in different areas, and take on broader responsibilities.

## Your Mission

Help every shopper, merchant, and creator have a positive, reliable, and supportive experience with ClothME while contributing insights that continuously improve our products and services.

## What You'll Do

### Shopper Support & Experience

Help consumers enjoy a smooth and confident shopping experience.

Responsibilities include:

- Respond to customer questions through email, chat, and other supported channels.
- Help customers navigate registration and account setup.
- Assist users with creating and managing Fit Profiles.
- Explain how fit-based product discovery works.
- Help customers understand product information and available sizing guidance.
- Support customers with account, shopping, and order-related questions.
- Assist with checkout, payment, and order-status inquiries.
- Help customers understand applicable returns, refunds, and cancellation policies.
- Investigate and escalate technical or transaction-related issues.
- Identify opportunities to improve the overall shopping experience.

### Merchant Support & Experience

Help fashion businesses use ClothME successfully.

Responsibilities include:

- Assist merchants with account registration and platform navigation.
- Explain brand verification and submission processes.
- Help merchants understand product publishing requirements.
- Support basic questions about product catalogs, sizing, variants, and inventory.
- Assist merchants with available integrations and account settings.
- Help troubleshoot common merchant dashboard issues.
- Coordinate complex merchant concerns with Merchant Partnerships and Engineering.
- Identify recurring merchant onboarding challenges.
- Contribute to merchant help articles and troubleshooting resources.
- Help maintain clear and timely communication with merchants.

### Creator Support & Experience

Support creators participating in ClothME's creator ecosystem through Thesi.

Responsibilities include:

- Assist creators with registration and account-related questions.
- Help creators understand available campaigns and participation requirements.
- Explain campaign application and submission processes.
- Support questions about product selection and creator links.
- Help creators understand available performance and attribution information.
- Route campaign, commission, or payment concerns to the appropriate team.
- Identify recurring creator experience issues.
- Collaborate with Creator Partnerships to improve creator onboarding.
- Contribute to creator support documentation.
- Help ensure creators receive clear, consistent information.

### Customer Support Operations

Help establish efficient and scalable support processes.

Responsibilities include:

- Manage and organize incoming support requests.
- Categorize customer issues and maintain accurate records.
- Follow established response and escalation procedures.
- Help develop support workflows and internal documentation.
- Identify opportunities to improve response and resolution times.
- Support the implementation of helpdesk and customer support tools.
- Maintain professional and consistent communication.
- Follow up on unresolved issues.
- Help improve support quality and consistency.
- Contribute to operational improvements as support volume grows.

### Customer Education & Self-Service

Help customers find answers and solve common problems independently.

Responsibilities include:

- Create and maintain frequently asked questions.
- Develop clear help articles and troubleshooting guides.
- Identify common support questions suitable for self-service resources.
- Help produce simple tutorials and instructional content.
- Collaborate with Product and Growth on onboarding communications.
- Review existing support content for clarity and accuracy.
- Recommend improvements to in-app guidance and help experiences.
- Support the development of a scalable customer knowledge base.

### Voice of the Customer

Help ensure customer feedback influences ClothME's development.

Responsibilities include:

- Collect and document customer feedback.
- Identify recurring complaints, questions, and product difficulties.
- Categorize feedback into actionable themes.
- Share relevant insights with Product and Engineering.
- Highlight opportunities to simplify onboarding and shopping.
- Help evaluate improvements to customer-facing features.
- Participate in testing new workflows when appropriate.
- Follow up with customers when reported issues are resolved.
- Help the team understand how product decisions affect real users.

### Customer Trust, Privacy & Escalations

Help protect customer confidence in ClothME.

Responsibilities include:

- Handle customer information responsibly and confidentially.
- Follow established data protection and account verification procedures.
- Recognize potential fraud, account security, and payment concerns.
- Escalate sensitive issues to authorized team members.
- Communicate clearly during service disruptions or technical incidents.
- Avoid making unsupported promises about refunds, orders, sizing, or product availability.
- Treat customers fairly and respectfully.
- Help identify opportunities to improve customer trust and safety.

### Reporting & Performance

Help build a measurable and continuously improving customer experience.

Responsibilities include:

- Track support requests and issue categories.
- Monitor response and resolution times.
- Collect customer satisfaction feedback.
- Identify recurring support trends.
- Prepare regular summaries of customer concerns.
- Report common product friction points.
- Recommend improvements based on support data.
- Help measure the effectiveness of self-service resources.
- Document recurring issues and their resolutions.

## Interview Process

Our interview process focuses on communication, problem-solving, customer empathy, technical understanding, and practical judgment.

**Stage 1 — Initial Conversation**

Discuss your background, customer support experience, and interest in ClothME.

**Stage 2 — Founder Interview**

Meet with the Founder to learn about ClothME's mission, customer experience expectations, and the challenges we're solving.

**Stage 3 — Customer Experience Discussion**

Walk us through examples of customer issues you've personally resolved and improvements you've helped introduce.

**Stage 4 — Practical Customer Scenario**

Complete a focused, job-relevant exercise involving a realistic shopper, merchant, or creator support situation. We aim to respect candidates' time and avoid unnecessarily lengthy assignments.

**Stage 5 — Collaboration & Values Interview**

Discuss your approach to teamwork, customer privacy, communication, and handling difficult situations.

**Stage 6 — References & Offer**

For shortlisted candidates, complete reference checks and discuss compensation, responsibilities, and next steps.
